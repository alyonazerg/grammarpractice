Future time clauses

When · After · Until · Before · Once · As soon as

What will you cook when you have more free time?

Notice the pattern

I'll chop the vegetables after you rinse them.
We'll wait until the water boils.

Which part refers to the future? Which tense follows the time expression?

The rule

When + present simple, will + verb.

When this food trend comes back in style, people will try it again.

Never use will immediately after when, after, until, before, once or as soon as in future time clauses.
