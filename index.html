Future time clauses

When · After · Until · Before · Once · As soon as

What will you cook when you have more free time?

Notice the pattern

I'll chop the vegetables after you rinse them.
We'll wait until the water boils.

Which part refers to the future? Which tense follows the time expression?

The rule

When + present simple, will + verb.

When this food trend comes back in style, people will try it again.

Never use will immediately after when, after, until, before, once or as soon as in future time clauses.

Controlled practice

1. After I the pineapple, I'll add it to the salad.
2. This fad will disappear when people interest.
3. We won't fry the fish until the oil hot.

Correct the mistakes

I'll stir the sauce when it will boil.
Once the dish will gain popularity, restaurants will serve it everywhere.

Free speaking

Plan a dinner with a friend who loves fashionable food.

What will you prepare before your guests arrive? Which ingredients will you try when they gain popularity? What will you do once everything is ready?

Use four future time clauses and at least five expressions: chop, rinse, boil, fry, stir, barbecue, trendy, be all the rage, go out of style, gain popularity.
